# Target temperature   : Which Temperature should the probe have (fE: 260°C)?

# measuring duration   : How long should the measerment take place? "2" means the measerment will take 2 minutes. pauses are not added.
# Stationary duration  : How long should be the stationary be measured? "2" means the temperature has to be stationary for 2 minutes.
# Stationary tolerance : How much deveation of the temperature is still considerd stationary? ".8" means that the lowest and higest temperature for the set Stationary duration can not be 0.8°C appart or the the temperature would not be considert stationary and the measerment does not start.
